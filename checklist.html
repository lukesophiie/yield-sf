      What this can't tell you
      
  
        
    
      • How often robots break these rules. Operators must report incidents and complaints to the City every month under §794(h)(2)(C), but those reports are not published. There is no public incident dataset to check against.
        • 
    
      • Exact speed and distance. A passer-by estimates 3 mph and 30 ft by eye. A failed check is grounds to report, not proof.
        • 
    
      • Zoning, corridor and permit status on the spot. Those three need a lookup on City maps or the Public Works permit list.
        • 
    
      • Extra conditions on a specific permit. The Director can add rules under §794(c) and (h)(1) that are not in the code text.
